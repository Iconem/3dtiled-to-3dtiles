## Why this exists

The [TODO / Future work](/docs/guide/todo-future-work) fastest-tiler research note flagged that **no
neutral benchmark existed** — not against PotreeConverter 2.0 / untwine, and not even run-over-run
against ourselves. This page is the first step: a pinned, reproducible harness measuring **our own
converters on fixed public datasets**, so any performance change (e.g. wiring the `WorkerPool`
multicore tier, or the WebP-instead-of-pngjs decode swap) has a before/after number.

## Running it

```sh
node scripts/benchmark-converters.mjs            # all datasets
node scripts/benchmark-converters.mjs --only potree,rad
```

Sources are mirrored once into `.bench-cache/` (download time reported separately and cached across
runs), then the **offline `convert()`** is timed cold per run. Results print as a markdown table and
are appended as dated JSON under `benchmark-results/`.

## First results (2026-08-22)

Machine: 12th Gen Intel Core i7-12800H × 20 threads · Node v22.22.3 · Windows 11.

| Dataset | Format | Source MB | Convert s | Output files | Output MB | Throughput |
|---|---|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| potree1-lion-takanawa | potree | 4.9 | 0.75 | 296 | 7.8 | 0.36 M points/s |
| potree2-lion | potree | 44.2 | 3.92 | 1741 | 111.9 | 1.01 M points/s |
| sog-skatepark | sog | 174.4 | 21.14 | 2055 | 165.8 | 8.3 MB/s |
| rad-tastier-500k | rad | 12.2 | 0.53 | 12 | 9.9 | 0.94 M splats/s |

Reading the numbers:

- **~1 M points/s (Potree 2.0)** on a single core (the `WorkerPool` tier isn't wired — see
  [Performance](/docs/guide/architecture-generic)) vs PotreeConverter 2.0's published **~9 M points/s**
  *for a different job*: it builds an octree from raw unordered points; our converter *transcodes an
  existing octree*. The honest comparison for the fastest-tiler ambition is our future raw-input
  tiler vs PotreeConverter/untwine — this table is the regression baseline for the transcode path.
- **SOG is decode-bound** (WebP → DataTable via WASM, then SPZ re-encode): 8.3 MB/s of source. This is
  the path with the most multicore headroom.
- **RAD is nearly free** (0.53 s for 500 k splats) — column decode + SPZ encode, no image codecs.

## External tilers (context, not apples-to-apples)

| Tool | Job | Their reported speed |
|---|---|---|
| [PotreeConverter 2.0](https://github.com/potree/PotreeConverter) | raw points → Potree octree | ~9 M pts/s uniform / ~6 M pts/s blue-noise (published paper) |
| [untwine](https://github.com/hobuinc/untwine) | raw points → COPC | 2–5× faster than `pdal translate` past 500 M pts (own claim) |
| [MIERUNE point-tiler](https://github.com/MIERUNE/point-tiler) | LAS/LAZ/CSV → 3D Tiles 1.1 | Rust/Rayon; no published numbers — worth measuring |

None of those transcode *already-tiled* formats (this repo's job today), and none of their numbers are
third-party-verified — see the TODO doc's research note. When the raw-input tiler direction is scoped,
these become the targets and this harness grows a raw-input track (same datasets as their READMEs use,
e.g. Autzen / CA13).
