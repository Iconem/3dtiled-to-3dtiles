Turn a tiled dataset, on this machine or at a URL, into OGC 3D Tiles. Nothing is uploaded: the conversion happens here.

Advanced format, materialization, placement on the globe

For sources with no georeference: applied as a root ENU transform in the tileset itself, so every client gets it. Sources that carry their own georeference ignore these.

Enter a dataset above, then stream it live or export it to a folder.
